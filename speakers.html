大会主题报告人

MT

Prof. My T. Thai

Professor, University of Florida, USA | IEEE Fellow

Title: Trustworthy AI, Network Science, and Combinatorial Optimization

Abstract: My T. Thai is a distinguished researcher whose work spans trustworthy AI, network science, and combinatorial optimization. Her research focuses on explainable AI, AI safety and privacy protection, large-scale complex graph data mining, and their innovative applications in blockchain, critical infrastructure cybersecurity, and healthcare. She has received the DTRA Young Investigator Award (2009), NSF CAREER Award (2010), and multiple best paper awards including IEEE ICDM 2017 and AAAI 2023 Distinguished Paper Award. She has published over 300 papers and 7 books, and serves as Editor-in-Chief of the Journal of Combinatorial Optimization and ACM Computing Surveys.

Bio: My T. Thai is a UF Research Foundation Professor in the Department of Computer & Information Science & Engineering and the Associate Director of the Nelms Institute for the Connected World at the University of Florida. She is an IEEE Fellow. Her research focuses on trustworthy AI, network science, and combinatorial optimization. She is the founding Editor-in-Chief of IET Blockchain and serves on the editorial board of the Springer Optimization and Its Applications book series.

SH

Prof. Seok-Hee Hong

Professor, University of Sydney, Australia | ARC Future Fellow

Title: Graph Drawing, Algorithms, and Information Visualization

Abstract: Seok-Hee Hong is a leading researcher in graph drawing, algorithms, and information visualization. She has achieved systematic breakthroughs in symmetric graph drawing, 1-planar graphs, and large-scale complex network visualization, proposing sublinear-time visualization algorithms and faithful visualization frameworks for billion-scale large complex graphs. She has deeply applied graph drawing theory and algorithmic innovations to social networks, bioinformatics networks, and public transit networks, driving the field from planar graph drawing to beyond-planar graph drawing.

Bio: Seok-Hee Hong is a Professor in the School of Computer Science at the University of Sydney, an ARC Future Fellow, and a Humboldt Fellow. She previously served on the ARC College of Experts. She was the project lead of NICTA VALACON for large complex network visualization and analysis, and serves on the steering committees of IEEE PacificVis and ISAAC. She is an editorial board member of the Journal of Graph Algorithms and Applications (JGAA) and is widely recognized as a leading scholar in graph drawing and visualization.

Prof. Guochuan Zhang

Professor, Zhejiang University, China

Title: Online Algorithms and Combinatorial Optimization

Abstract: Guochuan Zhang is one of the earliest researchers in online algorithms in China and has long been dedicated to the analysis and design of algorithms for combinatorial optimization. His research interests include discrete optimization, approximation algorithms, online algorithms, and algorithmic game theory. He has achieved a series of important algorithmic results on classic combinatorial optimization problems such as bin packing and scheduling. He was awarded the Humboldt Research Fellowship (2001), the Operations Research Award of the Chinese Operations Research Society (2018), and the Outstanding Teacher Award for Computer Science in Universities (2023).

Bio: Guochuan Zhang is a Professor in the College of Computer Science and Technology at Zhejiang University. He served as Vice President of the Operations Research Society of China (2020-2028) and is a member of the ISAAC Advisory Committee, the COCOON Steering Committee, and a founding member of the Asian Association for Algorithms and Computation (AAAC). He has published over 130 papers with more than 1,000 citations and serves on the editorial boards of Journal of Scheduling, Annals of Operations Research, International Journal of Foundations of Computer Science, and Journal of Combinatorial Optimization.

FK

Prof. Fernando A. Kuipers

Full Professor, TU Delft, Netherlands

Title: Next-Generation Network Optimization and AI-Driven Network Design

Abstract: Fernando A. Kuipers's research focuses on the performance and reliability of the Internet and communication infrastructures. He innovatively applies algorithms, optimization, and AI techniques to network protocol optimization, critical infrastructure robustness analysis, and next-generation network architecture design. He has received multiple best paper awards at top conferences including IEEE INFOCOM, IFIP Networking, and ITC. He was Co-PI of the Dutch 6G flagship project Future Network Services, leading its intelligent networking program line.

Bio: Fernando A. Kuipers is a Full Professor and head of the Networked Systems Group at TU Delft. He has been a visiting scholar at the Technion and Columbia University. He served as General Chair of ACM SIGCOMM 2021 and 2022, Program Committee Chair of IEEE INFOCOM 2024, and currently serves as Vice Chair of the ACM SIGCOMM Executive Committee.
